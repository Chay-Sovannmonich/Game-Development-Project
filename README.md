# ☕ ChillTown Café

ChillTown Café is a cozy **casual restaurant management simulation game** built with **Unity 3D**.  
You play as a new café owner in a small town, cooking food, serving customers, managing workers, and expanding your café into a successful business.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### 🏪 Starting the Game
- Launch the game from the **Main Menu**
- Click **Play** to enter the café
- You start with a **small café** and basic cooking equipment

---

### 🍳 Cooking Food
1. Walk to a **stove** or cooking machine
2. Open the **Cooking Menu**
3. Select a dish to cook
4. The machine will start a **countdown timer**
5. When the timer finishes, the food becomes **Ready**
6. Collect the food before time runs out

> ⚠️ Cooking machines are locked while cooking

---

### 🧑‍🍽️ Serving Customers
- Customers arrive and sit at tables
- Each customer has a **patience timer**
- Serve the correct food before they get impatient
- Faster service = **more coins & higher happiness**

---

### 💰 Rewards & Progression
- **Coins** are earned by serving food and completing tasks
- **EXP** is earned by cooking, cleaning, and serving customers
- Leveling up unlocks:
  - New recipes
  - New workers
  - Café upgrades

---

### 👨‍🍳 Workers
- Hire workers to help manage the café
- Workers can:
  - Take customer orders
  - Deliver food
  - Clean tables
- Workers move automatically using pathfinding

---

### 🛠️ Upgrades
Use coins to improve your café:
- Unlock new cooking machines
- Hire additional workers
- Add decorations to increase customer happiness
- Expand the café layout

---

### 📊 UI Overview
- **Coins Bar** – Shows your current money
- **EXP Bar** – Shows level progress
- **Customer Happiness Bar** – Indicates overall satisfaction
- **Menus**:
  - Cooking Menu
  - Upgrade Shop
  - Settings

---

## 🖥️ Controls (PC)
- **Mouse Click** – Interact with objects, menus, and machines
- **Mouse Scroll** – Zoom camera
- **W / A / S / D** (if enabled) – Move camera

---

## 🎯 Objective
Grow your café from a small shop into a popular restaurant by:
- Serving customers quickly
- Keeping happiness high
- Managing workers efficiently
- Upgrading and expanding your café
